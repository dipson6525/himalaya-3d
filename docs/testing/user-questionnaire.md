# User test and questionnaire (10+ users)

Owner: Dipson Gurung (with everyone) · Issue #31 · NFR11 · Proposal sections 1.4 and 3.8
("at least ten users, including people who have trekked before and people who have not, will try
the platform and answer a short questionnaire about usefulness and ease of use")

Printable version: [user-questionnaire-print.html](user-questionnaire-print.html) (open it in a
browser and print, A4).

## Before each session

- Use the live site on a laptop; note the laptop and browser in the session table.
- Read the consent line to the participant (below). Record a **participant ID** (P01, P02, ...),
  not a name. Do not record e-mail addresses, phone numbers or anything else that identifies them.
- Do not help unless the participant is stuck for more than about a minute; if you help, mark the
  task "with help".

> **Consent:** "This is a student project at Kathmandu University. We are testing the website, not
> you. It takes about 15 minutes. You can stop at any time. We will write down your answers under a
> number, not your name, and only use them in our project report."

## Part 5 — Open questions

1. What did you find most useful?
2. What was confusing or hard to find?
3. What would you add or change?
4. Would you use this to plan a real trek? Why or why not?

## Scoring SUS

For each participant: odd items (1, 3, 5, 7, 9) score = answer − 1; even items (2, 4, 6, 8, 10)
score = 5 − answer. Add the ten scores and multiply by 2.5. The result is 0–100 (it is **not** a
percentage). Scores around 68 are often treated as average (Sauro, J., 2011, *A Practical Guide to
the System Usability Scale*). Report the mean, the lowest and the highest, and the number of
participants.
